Hardware-grade security and full Stellar DeFi, in one wallet

Korean XLM holders today choose between safe storage and easy use. Exchanges are easy but custodial. Hardware wallets are safe but stop at send and receive. Software wallets reach Stellar DeFi but keep keys on a phone or browser. DCENT gives holders both.

What sets DCENT apart

Security first, so holders can actually use DeFi

Keys stay in a certified secure element (EAL5+ to EAL6+) across the DCENT lineup. Every dApp, swap and vault action is approved on the device, and official vault routes sit inside the app to keep users away from phishing sites.

Full Stellar support on a hardware wallet

Native Stellar dApps, Stellar and cross-chain swaps, and Soroban vaults (Sentora, Upshift by Gami) in one app. DCENT is the only hardware wallet supported by Sentora's Stellar vault.

Start with zero XLM

DCENT sponsors the base reserve and network fees, so a new holder can open a Stellar account, add trustlines and make a first move without buying XLM for gas.

A local playbook that already works

DCENT moved Korean XRP holders into on-chain vaults through XRP Alliance, ran Stellar Night Seoul with SDF, and works with Korean crypto creators who already cover XLM.
