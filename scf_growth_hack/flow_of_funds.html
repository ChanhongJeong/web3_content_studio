Flow of funds · Stellar

Every flow starts and ends in the user's own account

DCENT is a self-custody hardware wallet. DCENT never holds, pools or moves user funds. Users approve every transaction on their DCENT hardware wallet.

Into the account
Out and back

Fiat on-ramp

Licensed partner handles KYC and card or bank payment, then delivers to the user's address.

Exchanges and other wallets

Holders move XLM and Stellar assets from exchanges such as Upbit into self-custody.

DCENT sponsor account

Covers base reserve and network fees, so new users start with zero XLM. No control over the account.

DCENT campaign wallet

Sends campaign rewards (XLM, stablecoins or partner tokens) after on-chain verification, with a per-user cap.

Self-custody

User's own Stellar account

Across the DCENT hardware lineup, private keys stay in a certified secure element (EAL5+ to EAL6+). Nothing moves until the user approves it on the device.

Keys never leave the deviceApproved on device

Sentora vault

Soroban vault. The position stays with the user's address.

Upshift vault by Gami

Deposit and withdraw directly from the user's account.

Swap

SushiSwap and Squid. Stellar and cross-chain.

Stellar dApps

Opened natively in the DCENT in-app browser.

Any address or exchange

Pay, send to friends, or cash out.

Signed by the user on the DCENT device Paid by DCENT, never taken from users Sent by a partner or the user's other account

No custody

Balances stay in the user's own Stellar account or in contracts the user chose. DCENT cannot move them.

Safe access to Stellar DeFi

Official vault and dApp routes sit inside the app, and every action is checked on the device. This protects users from phishing and fake sites.

DCENT pays, never takes

DCENT's own funds go out only as network costs for zero-XLM starts and as campaign rewards. DCENT takes nothing from vault returns.

What DCENT never does

Hold user keysKeys are created and stored on the hardware wallet. DCENT has no copy and no recovery access.

Pool or route user fundsThere is no DCENT omnibus account. Every transaction goes straight from the user's address.

Take a cut of vault returnsVault returns go to the user. Campaign rewards are paid on top, from DCENT's own wallet.
